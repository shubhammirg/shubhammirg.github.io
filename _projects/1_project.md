A trimodal imaging platform that simultaneously captures cerebral hemodynamics and neural activity in the rodent brain by combining **functional ultrasound (fUS)**, **intrinsic optical signal (IOS)**, and **widefield calcium imaging**.

Key contributions:

- Established awake-mouse fUS imaging as a capability for the lab.
- Built the acquisition system on Verasonics Vantage and Vantage NXT systems, using Arduino-based TTL triggering to synchronize ultrasound, sCMOS/CMOS imaging cameras, and behavioral recording.
- Developed whisker and visual stimulus delivery for sensory-evoked imaging experiments.
- Designed a 3D-printed probe holder to integrate the ultrasound and optical imaging pathways.
- Cross-validated the fUS signal against intrinsic hemoglobin signals and derived transfer functions to characterize sensitivity and linearity.
- Demonstrated the platform's utility for multiscale imaging in a glioblastoma (GBM) mouse model.

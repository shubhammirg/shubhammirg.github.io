Development and characterization of **transparent ultrasound transducers** based on lead magnesium niobate-lead titanate (PMN-PT) and lithium niobate piezoelectric materials.

Key contributions:

- Characterized transducer performance and noise through pulse-echo, hydrophone pressure, and photoacoustic measurements, using PiezoCAD for modeling and MATLAB for data analysis.
- Demonstrated that PMN-PT is a viable alternative to lithium niobate for multimodal optical and photoacoustic imaging setups.
- Designed an electrical impedance matching circuit to improve transducer performance.

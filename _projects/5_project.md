Design and demonstration of **fiber-optic interferometric hydrophone sensor arrays** for ultrasound detection, forming the basis of the Master's thesis at IIT Kanpur.

Key contributions:

- Implemented a homodyne phase-generated carrier (PGC) scheme for passive interrogation, demonstrating successful recovery of the acoustic signal.
- Proposed and experimentally demonstrated a passive path imbalance measurement technique using the same homodyne PGC setup.
- Multiplexed a five-sensor interferometer array using Time Division Multiplexing.
